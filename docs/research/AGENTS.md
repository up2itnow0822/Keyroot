# Keyroot Research

## Purpose
- Preserve the source plan, research and concept-review evidence supporting Keyroot requirements.

## Ownership
- This folder owns historical inputs and evidence snapshots.
- `../plans/` owns accepted normative product behavior.

## Local Contracts
- Preserve imported source bytes; corrections and later interpretations belong in another artifact.
- Prior proposals and critique statements describe their composition-time state, not current owner acceptance or implementation status.
- Official documentation is evidence of documented capability, not a real-account permission or integration test.
- Record no secret values, live credential dumps or secret-bearing media.
- Raw evidence is local-only and ignored by Git. This contract may be published to explain that boundary.

## Work Guidance
- Cite exact source documents and dates; label inferences and untested claims.
- Read the plan before treating historical proposal language as an active requirement.

## Verification
- Check hashes when importing files and verify plan-to-evidence local references.
